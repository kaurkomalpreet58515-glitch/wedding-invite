PARAMJIT KAUR & MANJIT SINGH
1 January 2031

To host
Extract this ZIP and upload index.html, style.css, app.js and the assets folder together into your website's public folder. Keep index.html at the root of that folder. Open your website address.

To preview
Extract all files, then open index.html in Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.

Interaction
Scroll or swipe vertically through the invitation. Scrolling back revisits earlier cards. If music needs permission, tap once anywhere on the invitation.

Personalisation
The new Punjabi names and date are editable text in index.html.
Paramjit Kaur is the bride. Manjit Singh is the groom.
The supplied single date, 1 January 2031, is used for all ceremony cards.
Family names, venues and times follow the reference. The reference lists Anand Karaj at 10:30 AM on the formal invitation and 9 AM on its ceremony card. Confirm these details before sending to guests.

Artwork and music
This version uses 1080 x 1920 still card artwork extracted from the previously upscaled reference video, with editable text overlays and scroll-controlled sliding transitions. The source video was 720 x 1280. Background music is the uploaded MP3 and loops.
Original designer credit is included on the last card.
The Gurmukhi fonts are bundled locally. Their licence is in assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt.
